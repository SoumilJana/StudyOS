Pre-requisite – Basic knowledge of Computer Networking, Operating Systems (especially Linux), Basics of scripting in 
Python, Bash, 
 
Course Outcomes: 
CO1 Students will be able to understand the core concepts of of ethical hacking, cybersecurity threats, legal 
frameworks, and penetration testing methodologies. 
CO2 Students will be able to apply reconnaissance, scanning, enumeration, vulnerability assessment, and 
exploitation techniques using industry-standard tools. 
CO3 Students will be able to analyze vulnerabilities in operating systems, web applications, wireless networks, 
APIs, and cloud environments, and recommend suitable countermeasures..

CO4 Students will be able to conduct ethical penetration testing, prepare professional security assessment 
reports, and recommend mitigation strategies following industry standards.
